Annual and irregular expenses

The large and irregular expenses the monthly Planner does not carry: long-term care premiums, quarterly tax estimates, the big trip. This is where they are budgeted. Each year's total divided by twelve is added to the monthly expenses so the reserve and the transfer are smooth, and checking builds a float toward each one between due dates.

Each month

  1. Check the figures pulled from the Planner, then enter the money market and managed balances.
  2. Read the action line. If it says sell, place the sale in the managed account that is furthest ahead, taxable first.
  3. Carry the transfer figure into the weekly planner as the first-of-month deposit to checking.
  4. Save the month to the log.

The transfer is the same every month on purpose. Checking builds up a float between the annual payments and spends it when they land, so "Checking should hold" rises toward each due date and drops after it. Dividends and interest from the taxable managed account should be set to pay out to the money market, not reinvest, so the reserve refills partly on its own.
