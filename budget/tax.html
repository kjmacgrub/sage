The Plan

Steady conversions from two IRAs, sized so household MAGI stays under the first IRMAA tier. Not trying to empty the IRAs. Hilary converts her own number in 2026; from 2027 either a fixed amount or whatever room is left under the line after Ken's. Edit any year's amount in the table to override it.

Year by Year

Base MAGI is household income before any conversion, including RMDs once they start at 75; an RMD comes out first and cannot be converted. Room is how far the year sits under the IRMAA line after conversions; red means over. Roth contributions are after-tax and do not move MAGI. Ken's IRA is the PSFC lump sum once it arrives.

Each December

  1. Look up the year's income so far on the YTD Tracker tab, plus any muni interest.
  2. Take that away from this year's IRMAA line (hover the Room cell to see it). What is left is Hilary's conversion for the year, after Ken's.
  3. Type the real numbers into the Ken and Hilary cells for that year. Hand-entered years are outlined; clear a cell to return to the plan.

Medicare looks back two years: this year's MAGI sets premiums two years from now. Crossing the line by one dollar costs the whole first-tier surcharge for a year. Federal brackets and deductions are the 2026 figures indexed; the $6,000 senior deduction applies 2027 and 2028 and phases out at these incomes. NY exempts the first $20,000 of each person's pension, annuity or IRA money, so Ken's first $20,000 of conversions is the cheapest dollar in the plan. Social Security is taken as 85% taxable. RMDs start at 75, in 2037, from the Uniform Lifetime Table.
